ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Built around clarity, evidence and safety.

MedMatch AU explores whether medication record inconsistencies can be identified and presented more clearly to healthcare professionals.

What this prototype does

Demonstrates a modern document review interface with a synthetic patient case, staged progress presentation, discrepancy comparison, reviewer dispositions and local text export.

Australian context

The planned live pipeline will use appropriately licensed Australian Medicines Terminology, plus reference information from TGA and PBS where relevant.

What it does not do yet

No OCR, no LLM inference, no automatic parsing of selected files, no patient data uploads, and no clinical prescribing or treatment advice. The processing timeline is illustrative only.

Privacy and safety

Do not select actual patient documents in this public demonstration. Real clinical use requires privacy, security, regulatory and clinical evaluation before release.

Evidence supports review. It does not replace judgement.

Every flagged difference should be assessed by an appropriately qualified health professional.
