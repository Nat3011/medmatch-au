REFERENCE & SAFETY

Australian medicines context

Australian reference sources can help verify products and terminology, but they do not determine the correct regimen for an individual patient.

AMT

Australian Medicines Terminology

Standardised medicine names, ingredient and product concepts. Full terminology integration requires NCTS access and appropriate usage terms.

Explore terminology ↗
PBS

Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme

Australian subsidised medicine listings and restrictions; PBS listing is not a patient-specific prescribing standard.

Explore PBS ↗
TGA

Therapeutic Goods Administration

ARTG medicine product information and registered formulations. Registration does not establish clinical appropriateness.

Explore TGA ↗

Prototype safeguards

This version performs local, rule-based comparisons of extracted text. It is not a validated medical AI system and does not run a live clinical LLM or verify patient-specific prescribing instructions against PBS/TGA/AMT. It cannot determine whether a prescription is clinically correct. Do not upload identifiable patient information. Findings are prompts for qualified clinician or pharmacist review only.
