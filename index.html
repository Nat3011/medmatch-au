What this version can and cannot do

It performs browser-side text/OCR extraction, heuristic facility and document-type recognition, and a limited rule-based discrepancy screen. It is not an LLM-driven clinical understanding engine, and PBS/TGA/AMT are not queried live. Accuracy is not validated. Use fictional test cases only; never upload identifiable patient records to this public demonstration.
